Image Generation Instructions (Detailed Spec)
1. Canvas & Format
Purpose: Website header / hero banner
Aspect ratio: Extremely wide panoramic
Target crop: 2000 × 700 px (≈ 2.85:1)
Background: Transparent (alpha channel)
Composition bias: Horizontal, minimal vertical height
2. Scene Description
Create an abstract futuristic cityscape skyline designed for modern technology branding.
City spans left‑to‑right across the entire frame
Skyline height is intentionally shallow
Central cluster slightly taller, tapering outward symmetrically
No dominant vertical focal point that would break a banner layout
3. Architectural Style
Futuristic, clean, optimistic
Glass‑like towers with:
Smooth curves
Faceted edges
Light blue / white materials
No real‑world city references
No gritty or cyberpunk elements
4. Foreground Elements
Calm reflective water surface beneath the skyline
Subtle reflections (soft, not mirror‑sharp)
Thin flowing light lines / arcs across the lower third:
Represent connectivity, data flow
Semi‑transparent
Do not obscure the skyline
5. Elements to EXCLUDE (Critical)
❌ No globe
❌ No planets or spheres in the sky
❌ No flying cars
❌ No drones
❌ No people
❌ No text
❌ No UI overlays
❌ No sky gradients baked into background (must work on transparency)
6. Color Palette
Primary: Soft blues, white, silver
Accents: very light cyan highlights
Contrast kept low and elegant
Avoid dark shadows or heavy blacks (important for transparency use)
7. Lighting
High‑key lighting
Even illumination
No strong directional shadows
Slight glow on edges of buildings
Designed to sit cleanly on both light and dark backgrounds
